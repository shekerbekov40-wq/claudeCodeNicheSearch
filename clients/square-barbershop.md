## Vibe & visual style (most important)
The client wants **clean, dialed pages with their own vibe, not random memes.**
Style references he likes: @amirobarbershops, @rizhaya.boroda, @chep.cuts.ov
- Calm, confident, cinematic. The work speaks.
- **No:** memes, emoji on screen or in captions, trending meme audio, reaction faces, zoom punches, green screen, "tag your girlfriend" style bait.
- Camera: vertical, 24/25 fps, tripod or slow handheld, slow push-ins, close-ups of hands / clippers / comb / neckline.
- Light: one soft light at 45°, clean background, counter cleared.
- Color: one consistent grade (slightly warm, muted, soft contrast) across every post.
- Sound: original audio: real clipper/scissor sound + the barber's calm voiceover, or one slow instrumental at low volume.
- On-screen text: one line at a time, one clean sans font, same size/position, white, lowercase, no emoji.
- End card on every video: *Square Barbershop · [SUBURB], IL · booking in bio*
- Captions: 2–3 short sentences, no emoji, 3–5 hashtags (1 broad, 2 local, 1–2 niche), shop location tag.
- Covers: final result frame with the barber in it, same framing every time.

## Clients on camera

- Clients appear only as a **silent model** or in a **natural conversation** while seated in the chair.

## Audience (research, Sep 2026)
- Men 16–35 in Chicago + suburbs; many drive 15–40 min for a barber they trust.
- Pains: same cut every time, can't explain what they want, grows out badly, cowlicks / thinning / curly hair.
- Fears: fade too high before an event, being rushed, wasting money on a new barber.
- Desires: a cut that fits *their* head, easy to style, confidence.
- Objections: too far, too expensive, no availability, "will it work on my hair?"
- So each video shows the *reasoning* behind a cut, names the area, and ends with the booking link or the DM keyword `CONSULT`.

## Competitors (Chicagoland)
Mostly silent before/after clips with hype captions (@j_da_barberr Berwyn, Schaumburg shops, Jay the Barber in Aurora). The gap is a barber who **talks through the cut** in a clean, cinematic style. That's our position.
