# Square Barbershop: Niche & Instagram Research

**Goal:** Build local fame in Chicago and the Chicago suburbs, and turn views into booked chairs (leads).
**Language:** English
**Cadence:** 2 Reels/week

> **How this research was done:** From this environment Instagram and TikTok can only be *searched*, not opened or played. So this research uses what search engines have indexed: Chicagoland barbers' public captions, hashtags and discover pages, plus 2026 barber and Instagram marketing guides. Every reference link below is a real post or page. Before filming, spend about 15 minutes watching them to see the pacing.

---

## 1. Target audience (ЦА): pains, fears, desires, objections

**Main audience:** men aged 16–35 in Chicago and the suburbs (students, young professionals, dads). Many drive 15–40 minutes for a barber they trust.
**Second audience:** girlfriends, wives and moms. They send Reels to "their guy", often book for him, and buy gift cards.

| | What they feel |
|---|---|
| **Pains** | "Every barber gives me the same cut." Can't explain what they want. Cut looks good for 3 days and then grows out badly. Thinning or receding hairline, cowlicks, curly hair nobody knows how to cut. |
| **Fears** | Walking out with a fade that's too high or a bad line-up before a date, interview or wedding. Being rushed. Wasting $40–60 and a Saturday. Trying a new barber and "betraying" the old one. |
| **Desires** | Looking like the guy in the reference photo. A cut they can style in 60 seconds. A barber who *thinks* about their head shape. Confidence, compliments, looking good in photos. |
| **Objections** | "Too far." "Probably expensive." "Can't get an appointment." "My barber is fine." "Will it work on *my* hair?" |

**What this means for content:** Every video should show the *thinking* behind a cut (that answers "will it work on my hair?"). Name suburbs and neighborhoods (answers "too far"). End with one easy action, a DM keyword or the booking link (answers "can't get an appointment").

**The gap for Square Barbershop:** Local competitors mostly post *silent* transformations. Almost nobody **talks through the cut** (разборы), does **celebrity breakdowns with a Chicago angle**, or shows **the founder**. If the barber is on camera explaining the cut, the page gets both the reach of transformation videos and the trust that personality builds. That trust is what turns viewers into bookings.

## 3. Formats that work, and how they map to our segments

What performs in the barber niche in 2026 (from the sources below):

1. **Transformation with a reveal.** 15–30 s of the cut plus a final reveal is still the best-performing barber format. Clipper sound, motion and the reveal keep people watching. Start *mid-cut*; never open on setup.
2. **Educational "what to ask your barber".** Gets saved and sent, and makes the barber look like the expert (e.g., low-taper explainers like [@barberteaux](https://www.instagram.com/barberteaux/reel/C7ArlkDInzc/)).
3. **Celebrity haircut breakdowns.** People search for these. Fall 2026 looks: Brad Pitt's warrior cut, the '70s mop, natural texture with a softer fade.
4. **Founder / "why I opened my shop".** Builds trust; pin it to the top of the profile (e.g., [@_619barbershop "Why I opened my own shop"](https://www.instagram.com/_619barbershop/reel/C0fHrz9RtcK/)).
5. **Relationship / "she booked it for him".** Broad content that gets sent in DMs.

| Consultation segment (рубрика) | Format it uses | Content type |
|---|---|---|
| Breakdown during a haircut | #1 + barber voiceover explaining decisions | Expert + selling |
| Celebrity breakdowns | #3 with a Chicago angle (The Bear, local teams) | Broad (beauty) + expert |
| Founder's story | #4 | Personal |
| Hairstyle breakdowns | #2 | Expert |
| (backup) Relationship transformation | #5 | Broad (relationships) + selling |

## 4. Instagram rules for local reach (2026)

- **The algorithm weighs three things most:** watch time, likes per reach, and **sends per reach**. DM shares count most for reaching new people. Write endings that get people to send the video ("send this to the guy who…").
- **Location tag on every post:** the shop's own location. On street or out-of-shop content, tag the suburb or neighborhood.
- **Caption keywords matter more than hashtags now.** Write natural sentences with "[Suburb] barber", "Chicago barber", "barber near [neighborhood]" in them. Use only **3–5 hashtags**: 1 broad (`#barber`), 2 local (`#chicagobarber #[suburb]barber`), 1–2 niche (`#lowtaper #texturedcrop`).
- **Say the place out loud and put it on screen.** "Chicago", the suburb, the client's hometown. Instagram reads on-screen text and captions for search.
- **Collab posts:** invite the client as a collaborator on their transformation. The Reel then also shows to their followers, who are mostly local friends.
- **Trial Reels** (once the page reaches 1,000 followers): test two hooks for the same video with non-followers first.
- **Cross-post** every Reel to TikTok and to the shop's Google Business Profile. Google Maps drives a lot of "barber near me" bookings.

---

## 5. Profile setup (упаковка)

Profile visits are where leads are won or lost. Before the first post:

- **Name field** (searchable): `[Barber Name] | [Suburb] Barber`, e.g. "Alex | Naperville Barber".
- **Bio:**
  ```
  💈 Square Barbershop — [Suburb], IL
  Tapers • fades • textured cuts
  Every cut starts with a consultation
  Serving Chicago + suburbs ↓ Book
  ```
- **Link:** straight to the booking page (Booksy / Squire / Square / theCut), not a link-tree with 8 options.
- **Highlights:** Book • Prices • Before/After • Reviews • Location (show parking).
- **Pinned posts (3):** Video 3 (founder story), the best transformation, and a price/booking post.
- **Auto-reply** for DM keywords (`CONSULT`, `CUT`), sending the booking link right away.
