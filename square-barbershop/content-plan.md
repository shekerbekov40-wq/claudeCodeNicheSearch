## VIDEO 1 · Sat Sep 26 · Breakdown during a haircut

**Script (title):** He drove from [CLIENT TOWN] for this: consultation + cut breakdown
**Goal:** Leads. Show the thinking behind a cut so viewers trust him, then collect DMs with the keyword "CONSULT". Naming the client's town reaches another suburb.
**Content type:** Expert + selling · **Length:** 30–40 s
**Reference:** https://www.tiktok.com/@j_da_barberr/video/7369768645339254059

**Pick the client:** someone with an obvious "before": grown out, a bad previous cut, a cowlick, or a thinning hairline. Ideally from a *different* suburb than the shop.

| Time | Visual | Voiceover / on-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | **Before** shot, client looking at the camera, unhappy | TEXT: *"Same haircut since high school 😐"*. VO: "He drove 35 minutes from [CLIENT TOWN] because every barber gives him the same cut." |
| 2–6 s | Barber's hands on the client's head, pointing | VO: "Before I touch the clippers, here's what I'm looking at." |
| 6–14 s | Clippers on the sides (real sound) | TEXT: *"1. Head shape"*. VO: "His head is wider at the temples. A high fade makes that look wider, so we're going low taper." |
| 14–20 s | Comb lifting the crown | TEXT: *"2. Cowlick"*. VO: "He's got a cowlick right here. I leave weight on it so it lies flat instead of sticking up by Wednesday." |
| 20–27 s | Scissors / texturizing on top | TEXT: *"3. Texture on top"*. VO: "Texture on top so he can style it in 60 seconds with a little matte paste." |
| 27–33 s | **Reveal:** chair spin, client's reaction, a handheld mirror | No VO, just music + his reaction. TEXT: *"Worth the drive?"* |
| 33–38 s | Barber to camera | VO: "Chicago and the suburbs: if your barber hasn't changed your cut in three years, **DM me 'CONSULT'** and I'll tell you what I'd change before you even book." |

**Caption:**
> He drove in from [CLIENT TOWN] because every barber gave him the same cut. Low taper + textured top, built around his head shape and crown cowlick. 💈
> Chicago / [SUBURB] barber · Every cut starts with a consultation.
> DM "CONSULT" or book through the link in bio.

**Hashtags:** `#chicagobarber #[suburb]barber #lowtaper #barbertransformation`
**Filming notes:** Tripod at a 45° angle behind the barber, phone vertical, clip-on mic on the barber, film the "before" *before* the cape goes on. Get the client's OK to post and to tag them as a Collab.

## VIDEO 2 · Tue Sep 29 · Celebrity breakdown

**Script (title):** Carmy's haircut from *The Bear*, explained by a Chicago barber
**Goal:** Local fame. *The Bear* is set and filmed in Chicago, so Chicago people share it ("a Chicago barber did this"). A celebrity breakdown also shows up in search beyond the page's followers.
**Content type:** Broad (beauty) + expert · **Length:** 35–45 s
**Reference:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BzsYOaIL4kU

**Setup:** Green-screen style. The barber stands in front of a still of Jeremy Allen White as Carmy, using the CapCut/Instagram green-screen effect. If a client with wavy or curly hair is booked, add 5–8 s of doing the cut on him.

| Time | Visual | Voiceover / on-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Barber in front of the Carmy photo | TEXT: *"Chicago barber breaks down Carmy's hair"*. VO: "The Bear is filmed in Chicago, so let a Chicago barber tell you how to get Carmy's hair." |
| 3–10 s | Points at the sides | TEXT: *"1. It's NOT a fade"*. VO: "First, it's not a fade. It's a scissor cut. The sides stay long enough to touch the top of the ears." |
| 10–18 s | Points at the top / crown | TEXT: *"2. Layers remove bulk"*. VO: "The top is layered to take out bulk. Otherwise wavy hair turns into a triangle." |
| 18–26 s | Holds up sea-salt spray / diffuser (shop products if possible) | TEXT: *"3. Styling = 90% of it"*. VO: "The look is in the styling: sea-salt spray on damp hair, air-dry or diffuse, and don't shampoo every day." |
| 26–32 s | Serious face, finger wag | TEXT: *"Won't work if…"*. VO: "If your hair is pin-straight and fine, this will fall flat. Ask for a textured crop instead." |
| 32–40 s | Text card | TEXT: *"Tell your barber: 'Scissor cut, keep the length, layer the top, take out bulk, no fade.'"* VO: "Screenshot this for your barber, or come see me in [SUBURB]." |
| 40–44 s | Barber to camera | VO: "**Comment the next celebrity haircut** you want me to break down." |

**Caption:**
> How to get Carmy's hair from The Bear, from a barber who actually cuts in Chicago. 🐻 Save this for your next appointment.
> Scissor cut · keep the length · layer the top · remove bulk · no fade.
> Square Barbershop, [SUBURB] IL · Book via link in bio.

**Hashtags:** `#chicagobarber #thebear #jeremyallenwhite #texturedhair #[suburb]barber`
**Filming notes:** Use your own screenshot or photo for the green screen, not the show's footage (copyright), and don't use show music. Use the comments to pick future celebrity breakdowns: Brad Pitt's "warrior cut" and the '70s mop are the big fall 2026 requests, and Chicago athletes' cuts are another local angle.

## VIDEO 3 · Fri Oct 2 · Founder's story

**Script (title):** How I built Square Barbershop: from my first haircut to my own chair in [SUBURB]
**Goal:** Trust + follows. People book people. This becomes **pinned post #1**: new profile visitors who came from Videos 1–2 watch it and decide to book.
**Content type:** Personal · **Length:** 45–60 s
**Reference:** https://www.instagram.com/_619barbershop/reel/C0fHrz9RtcK/

**Before filming, interview the barber** (10 min, phone voice memo) and build the voiceover from his *real* answers:
1. When and where did you do your first haircut? On who?
2. What were you doing before barbering? What made you switch?
3. What was the hardest moment (money, no clients, moving, family doubting you)?
4. The day you realized "this is going to work"?
5. Why the name "Square"?
6. What does your shop do differently from the barbers around you?
7. Who do you want sitting in your chair?
8. Any old photos or videos: first clippers, first client, empty shop before opening?

**Structure (fill in with his answers):**

| Time | Visual | Voiceover / on-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Old photo / first clippers, then cut to him today in the shop | TEXT: *"[X] years ago I cut my first head of hair in [place]."* VO: same line, then "Today I own Square Barbershop." |
| 3–15 s | Old photos, B-roll | **Where it started:** his origin in 2–3 sentences. *(If he moved to the US, lead with that. Immigrant-to-owner stories do very well in Chicago.)* |
| 15–28 s | Slower music, a moment of struggle | **The hard part:** one specific moment, e.g. "Some days I had two clients" or "My family wanted me to [other job]." |
| 28–38 s | Turning the lights on / opening the door / sweeping hair | **Turning point + why "Square":** VO: "I called it Square because [his real reason]." |
| 38–50 s | Him cutting, talking with a client, laughing | **What he stands for:** "Every cut here starts with a consultation. I don't do the same haircut on everybody." |
| 50–58 s | Barber to camera, in the chair or at the door | VO: "If you're in [SUBURB] or anywhere in Chicagoland, come sit in my chair. Link's in my bio." |

**Caption:**
> [X] years ago: first haircut in [place]. Today: Square Barbershop in [SUBURB]. 💈 This is why every cut here starts with a conversation.
> Thank you to every client who took a chance on me.

**Hashtags:** `#chicagobarber #[suburb] #smallbusiness #barbershop`
**Filming notes:** Film the voiceover *separately* in a quiet room, reading slowly. Use real photos where possible, even low-quality ones. Pin it to the profile right after posting.

## VIDEO 4 · Tue Oct 6 · Hairstyle breakdown

**Script (title):** Stop asking for "just a fade": low taper vs mid vs high vs burst in 30 seconds
**Goal:** Saves + sends ("send this to your boy before his next cut"), which gets the video shown to new people. It also shows the barber as the expert, so profile visits turn into bookings.
**Content type:** Expert · **Length:** 25–35 s
**Reference:** https://www.instagram.com/barberteaux/reel/C7ArlkDInzc/

**Setup:** Best option: 4 photos or clips of real Square Barbershop clients, one per fade. Quick option: one client or a mannequin head, with the barber holding a comb where each fade *starts*.

| Time | Visual | Voiceover / on-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | Barber holding clippers, looking at the camera | TEXT: *"'Just a fade' = 4 different haircuts"*. VO: "Tell a barber 'just a fade' and you could walk out with any of these four." |
| 2–8 s | Photo/clip #1, comb below the ear | TEXT: *"LOW TAPER: sideburns + neckline only"*. VO: "Low taper: only the sideburns and the neckline. Cleanest, safe for the office." |
| 8–14 s | Photo/clip #2, comb at the temple | TEXT: *"MID FADE: starts at the temple"*. VO: "Mid fade starts at the temple. Most popular, works on most head shapes." |
| 14–20 s | Photo/clip #3, comb above the temple | TEXT: *"HIGH FADE: above the temple"*. VO: "High fade: sharp, but you'll be back every two weeks." |
| 20–26 s | Photo/clip #4, comb curving around the ear | TEXT: *"BURST: curves around the ear"*. VO: "Burst fade curves around the ear. Goes with a mullet or mohawk." |
| 26–32 s | Barber to camera | VO: "Screenshot this for your next cut, or book with me in [SUBURB] and I'll tell you which one fits *your* head." TEXT: *"Send this to your boy 👇"* |

**Caption:**
> Low taper vs mid fade vs high fade vs burst fade, explained by a Chicago barber. Save this so you never have to say "just a fade" again. 💈
> Not sure which one fits your head shape? Book a cut in [SUBURB] (link in bio) and we'll figure it out in the consultation.

**Hashtags:** `#lowtaper #midfade #burstfade #chicagobarber #[suburb]barber`
**Filming notes:** Hold each photo for about 5 s so the text is readable. Put the text in the top half of the frame (Instagram's buttons cover the bottom). Consider also posting it as a carousel of 4 photos later in the week, since educational carousels get saved.

## After each post (10 minutes)

1. Reply to every comment within the first hour; replying keeps the post getting shown.
2. Send each "CONSULT" DM the booking link and one question ("What's your hair doing that you don't like?").
3. Share the Reel to Stories with a "Book" link sticker.
4. After 48 h, write down views, **sends**, saves, profile visits, and DMs/bookings in the Notion row. Whichever of the four segments brings the most DMs gets a second video in week 3.
